# Data Availability

Public expression and clinical data are available from GEO and TCGA/GDC under their original accessions and data-use terms. Derived audit tables, source data, supplementary data and figure data are available in the archived GitHub/Zenodo repository under the concept DOI https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20507011. Raw third-party expression matrices are not redistributed.

The final PubMed/Embase/Web of Science reconciliation outputs are included in `source_data/`:

- `all_databases_pre_dedup.csv`
- `all_databases_unique_records.csv`
- `all_databases_duplicate_records.csv`
- `all_three_databases_pre_dedup.csv`
- `all_three_databases_unique_records.csv`
- `all_three_databases_duplicate_records.csv`
- `deduplication_log_final.xlsx`
- `search_reconciliation_with_original_178.xlsx`

Users should obtain public raw expression matrices and clinical annotations from GEO, TCGA/GDC or the original source repositories under their applicable access terms.

The authoritative final manuscript data snapshot is retained as `supplementary/Appendix_3_Data.zip` and unpacked under `source_data/final_appendix3/` for direct inspection.
